# Support

Use a [bug report](https://github.com/dvmkit/dvm-cli/issues/new/choose) for a reproducible defect in the `dvm` CLI. Include the exact command, operating system and architecture, shell, `dvm` and Node.js versions, minimal reproduction, expected behaviour, and actual behaviour.

Use [Discussions](https://github.com/dvmkit/dvm-cli/discussions) for ideas, design questions, usage patterns, and requests without a reproduction. Documentation errors belong in a documentation report. Suspected vulnerabilities must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), never a public issue or discussion.

Email [support@dvmkit.com](mailto:support@dvmkit.com) for account access, billing, or a private problem with the hosted dvmkit service. Do not email wallet recovery material or plaintext credentials.

Community support has no response-time or service-level commitment and is not paid support. Maintainers can investigate the CLI and official service interfaces it calls; you remain responsible for your wallet credentials and spending, local data, device, network, shell environment, and third-party services.

Unsolicited pull requests are closed automatically. [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) explains what to send instead.
