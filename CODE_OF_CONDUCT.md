# Code of Conduct

Participation in this repository's issues and discussions must be respectful, professional, and free from harassment. Do not discriminate against, threaten, insult, or publish private information about another person.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when conduct violates this policy. Report conduct concerns privately through the support address in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md), with the subject `dvm CLI code of conduct report`; reports will be handled with reasonable confidentiality.
